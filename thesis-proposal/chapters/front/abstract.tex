\begin{preface}{Abstract}
  The advent of machine learning and edge computing has revolutionized how motion tracking applications are developed and deployed, enabling real-time, energy-efficient, and accurate systems for diverse use cases. This proposal outlines a research study aimed at building a framework to generate AI models tailored for motion tracking applications. The framework will enable users to easily collect motion data, train models, and evaluate their performance using tools such as Edge Impulse and Google MediaPipe.
  
  Focusing on the Seeed XIAO nRF52840 Sense as the primary hardware platform, this research explores its capabilities for motion detection and BLE communication to smartphones. By leveraging this device's onboard IMU sensor and its compatibility with edge AI tools, the study aims to demonstrate a scalable and practical solution for motion tracking. The research also investigates the challenges of real-time motion tracking, such as data quality, model optimization, and integration with resource-constrained edge devices.
  
  The proposed framework seeks to bridge the gap between complex machine learning workflows and non-expert users, empowering them to build and deploy motion tracking applications efficiently. The findings and methodologies presented in this study will lay the groundwork for the future development of motion tracking systems in areas such as healthcare, sports analytics, and robotics.
\end{preface}
